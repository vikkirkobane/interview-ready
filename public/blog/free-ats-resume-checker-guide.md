## What an ATS Actually Does (and Why It Filters Freshers)

Large employers, from global tech companies to banks and consultancies, receive thousands of applications for every role. ATS software scans resumes before a recruiter reads them, looking for three things: relevant keywords from the job description, clean parseable formatting, and complete standard sections【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】.

For freshers, the rejection is rarely about lacking experience. It is almost always about three fixable killers:
1. **No dedicated skills section**, skills buried inside project descriptions don't get scored by ATS parsers
2. **Generic objective statements**, "Seeking a challenging position where I can grow" contains zero keywords from the job description
3. **Formatting mistakes**, beautiful multi-column, graphic-heavy templates from Canva or Pinterest are unreadable to ATS parsers【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】

All three are fixable in under an hour.

## What a Free ATS Resume Checker Measures

A good free checker evaluates the same three factors that determine whether your resume passes automated screening: keyword match against a specific job description, formatting parsability, and section completeness【https://cvcraft.roynex.com/blog/best-free-resume-checker-2026】.

When you run your resume through a checker, look for:
- **An overall ATS score (0–100)**, for freshers, 70+ is considered good, and 85+ is excellent; most companies set their threshold around 60–70%【https://freshershunt.in/free-resume-ats-checker/】
- **Missing keywords list**, the exact terms from the job description your resume is missing

Freshers typically score 30–55 on their first check. That is normal, and fixable【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】.

## The ATS-Friendly Fresher Resume Checklist

Before submitting any application, run through this checklist:

**Formatting**
- Single-column layout (no multi-column, tables, or infographics)
- No photos, icons, or graphics
- Standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman (10–12pt)
- File format: .docx or simple text-based PDF
- One page for freshers with under a year of experience
- All text in the main body, nothing in headers or footers

**Content**
- Dedicated SKILLS section listing your technical skills explicitly
- Tailored summary/objective including the job title and 2–3 keywords from the posting
- PROJECTS with project name, technology stack, your role, and a measurable outcome
- EDUCATION with degree, institution, year, and CGPA
- At least one relevant certification【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】

**Keywords**
- The job title from the posting appears in your summary
- Required skills from the job description are in your skills section
- Your project technologies match the role's tech stack

The key rule: if a template looks impressive with its design, it probably fails ATS. The simpler and plainer the format, the better it parses【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】.

## What to Put on a Fresher Resume When You Have "No Experience"

No work experience does not mean an empty resume. ATS systems score what you put in. For freshers, the sections that matter most are:

- **Projects** (your most important section), every project counts: assignments, personal projects, college projects, hackathons. Format each as: Project Name | Technology Stack | Your Role | Outcome
- **Internships**, even a one-month internship counts as experience; write it with bullet points and results
- **Certifications**, AWS Cloud Practitioner, Google Data Analytics, Meta Frontend Developer, Microsoft AZ-900, these signal initiative and add keywords
- **Academic achievements**, CGPA (if 8+), class rank, scholarships, relevant coursework
- **Competitive programming**, LeetCode profile, CodeChef rating, HackerRank badges for tech roles
- **Extracurriculars with transferable skills**, student club president → leadership; hackathon participation → problem-solving【https://www.atsresumeschecker.com/fresher-resume-ats-checker】

## Why This Matters for Your Whole Job Search

Passing the ATS is just the first gate, it gets your resume in front of a human. The next gates are the recruiter screen and the interview itself. And here is the connection most job seekers miss: the same keywords that get you past the ATS are the topics your interviewers will ask about. If your resume says you know Python, SQL, and REST APIs, expect questions about them. If you list a project, expect to be asked what you built, what role you played, and what you learned.

This is where practice matters. The more you rehearse talking about the skills and projects on your resume, out loud, under realistic conditions, with feedback, the more naturally you answer when a recruiter asks about them. Interview preparation is the bridge between a resume that passes software screening and a candidate who passes the human conversation.
